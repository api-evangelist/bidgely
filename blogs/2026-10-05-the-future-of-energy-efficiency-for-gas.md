Electrification continues to dominate the energy efficiency narrative, with policy attention and public funding advancing the idea that the cleanest path forward runs entirely on electricity. The truth is that electrification is an important piece of the clean energy puzzle, but it isn’t the whole picture. Gas is also an integral part of the energy solution we need.
